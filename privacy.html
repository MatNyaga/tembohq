Website and newsletter privacy

Last updated 6 October 2026

This page covers tembohq.com and the Tembo email newsletter. The Tembo app has its own privacy policy: Tembo app privacy policy.

Who we are

Terra Softworks Limited makes Tembo. Contact: hello@tembohq.com.

The website

  • No cookies, no analytics and no ad trackers. Fonts, pictures and the video are served from this site itself.
  • The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses in its server logs for security, as its own privacy statement explains. We don't receive or use those logs.

The newsletter

If you sign up on this site, we send you Tembo news and general money-text tips, about two emails a month. We only add you after you tick the consent box and confirm with the link in our first email (double opt-in).

  • What we keep: your email address, when and where you signed up, and your confirmation, so we can show you agreed. Our email service also records whether an email was opened and which links were clicked, so we can tell which emails are useful.
  • Why: only to send you the newsletter, because you asked for it (your consent). We never sell your address, never share it for others' marketing, and never use it for anything else.
  • Who handles it for us: MailerLite (UAB "MailerLite", Lithuania), our email service. Its data is stored in the European Union.
  • How long: until you unsubscribe. After that we keep only your email address on a "do not email" list, so we never email you again by mistake.

Unsubscribing

Every email has a free, one-click unsubscribe link that stops all our emails. You can also email hello@tembohq.com. We act on every request at once, and always within 14 days.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019 you can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you, and object to direct marketing at any time. Email hello@tembohq.com. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (odpc.go.ke).

Children

The newsletter is for adults (18 and over). Please don't sign up if you're younger.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too. Big changes are announced in the newsletter.
